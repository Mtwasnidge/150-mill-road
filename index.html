A world of its own

Arched cedar ceilings inside. Four acres of forest and garden outside.

Built in 2012, this chalet sits at the heart of a property that has been shaped, garden by garden, into a private retreat.

Inside, the great room rises to a 24-foot arched wood ceiling, anchored by a fireplace built from Island stone. The kitchen, dining and living areas flow into one another under all that wood, and big south-facing windows pull in light and views of the land.

Outside is where it gets remarkable. A swimming pond, a wood-fired sauna, a hot tub, two bunkhouses, a 1,000 sq ft geodesic dome and a covered stage with power. The home comes fully furnished, has brought in about $30,000 a summer as an Airbnb, and includes a separate cottage lot that is already subdivided and approved.
